Prev/next buttons bake their target offset in server-side, so a click just
sets `$offset` and re-fetches. The endpoint re-renders the whole
`#pagination` region — list, buttons (with correct disabled states), and
page info — with the same Astro component the page used for the initial
render. `?page=` deep-links, and `data-replace-url` keeps it in sync.
